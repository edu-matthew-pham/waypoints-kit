# Review sheet (v0.2 draft)

For a colleague checking the kit's reading of the framework. About 20 minutes. Nothing here is final; mark up whatever you disagree with.

## 1. Read `core.md` (one page)
It is a condensed summary of the XY Model written for this kit. It was written from the framework's ideas, not copied from its source documents, so it may have drifted. Please check:
- Are the definitions right: spine (Y), width (X), minimum / typical / wider, hinge, width opportunity?
- Is "hinge vs consolidation" stated correctly?
- Are the five response modes described fairly?
- Is the pace profile (Consolidating / Steady / Advancing) described as a planning hypothesis, not an ability label?
- Does "What always holds" miss a rule you'd want, or include one you wouldn't?

## 2. Try it
Paste a starter prompt from `README.md` into any AI chat (use your own standard). Look at the first answer and a plan:
- Does the advice follow the node file's minimum width, hinge reasons and width opportunities?
- Does widening ever teach the *next* waypoint early?
- Does the tone read as a suggestion you can change, not an instruction?
- Is anything invented that isn't in the node file?

## 3. Known questions (decide, or tell us it doesn't matter)
1. **Pairing waypoints when lessons are lost.** `plan-format.md` suggests pairing adjacent waypoints (e.g. 3 and 4, or 4 and 5) "rather than shortening a hinge". Pairing a hinge with a neighbour may itself shorten it. Keep as a starting suggestion, or reword?
2. **Glossary examples that run ahead.** Some pre-built examples mention an idea from a later waypoint (e.g. the Waypoint 1 "Force diagram" example in AC9S7U04 mentions acceleration). The kit now flags these to the teacher and leaves them unchanged. Is that the right call?
3. **One-lesson waypoints with a practical.** When a plan squeezes teaching and a practical into one lesson, the kit notes the risk and offers a fallback. Enough?
4. **Pace profile as a starting hypothesis.** The kit assumes Steady unless told otherwise and says so. Acceptable?
