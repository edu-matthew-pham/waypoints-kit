## The kingdoms and what we can observe

| Kingdom | What we can observe |
|---|---|
| Animals | Move; take in food; made of many cells |
| Plants | Usually green; fixed in place; make their own food using light |
| Fungi | Absorb food from what they grow on; threads or mushroom shapes |
| Protists | Mostly single-celled; need a microscope; very varied |
| Bacteria | Single-celled; no nucleus; smallest of all |

## Which kingdom? Give one observable feature.

- Kangaroo
- Mushroom
- Gum tree

* Kangaroo: Animals (moves, takes in food)
* Mushroom: Fungi (absorbs food from what it grows on)
* Gum tree: Plants (green, fixed, makes its own food)
* Swimming cell: Protist (single-celled, seen under a microscope)

## Take away

> We place organisms into kingdoms by features we can **observe**, and we say which feature we used.
